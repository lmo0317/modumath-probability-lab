What comes after 2.5 overs?

೨.೫ ಓವರ್‌ಗಳ ನಂತರ ಇನ್ನೊಂದು ಮಾನ್ಯ ಎಸೆತವಾದರೆ ಸ್ಕೋರ್‌ಬೋರ್ಡ್ ಏನು ತೋರಿಸುತ್ತದೆ?

One over contains six valid deliveries. A wide or a no-ball does not add to that count. Try the three questions in order. You can enter 0–9 or ೦–೯.

From deliveries to the scoreboard

17 valid balls have been bowled. Write the scorecard notation in overs.

೧೭ ಮಾನ್ಯ ಎಸೆತಗಳನ್ನು ಓವರ್‌ಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಹೇಗೆ ಬರೆಯುತ್ತೀರಿ?

See the working · ಲೆಕ್ಕ ನೋಡಿ

17 = 2 × 6 + 5. The scorecard shows 2.5. The final digit counts balls. As an ordinary number of overs, this is 2 + 5/6.

One more valid ball

Starting from 2.5, one more valid ball is bowled. Write both parts of the new scorecard reading.

ಇನ್ನೊಂದು ಮಾನ್ಯ ಎಸೆತವಾದ ನಂತರ ಓವರ್‌ಗಳ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ ಎಷ್ಟು?

18 = 3 × 6 + 0. All six balls in the over are complete, so the display rolls over to 3.0.

Read it backwards

A different scorecard shows 7.2 overs. How many valid deliveries have been bowled?

೭.೨ ಓವರ್‌ಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಎಷ್ಟು ಮಾನ್ಯ ಎಸೆತಗಳಿವೆ?

Seven completed overs account for 42 valid balls. Add the two in the current over to get 44.
