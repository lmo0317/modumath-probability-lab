Why two shuttle routes settle into a 2:1 split

A campus runs routes A and B. In a simplified weekday model, three quarters of yesterday’s A passengers return to A, while half of yesterday’s B passengers move to A. There are 768 regular passengers. To start, everyone uses A. Can you predict the counts two days later without multiplying out a matrix power?

Read each column as a starting route

TomorrowFrom A todayFrom B today
Route A3/41/2
Route B1/41/2

Call this matrix M. Store counts in a column x = (A, B)T. Then xn+1 = Mxn. Each column sums to 1, so tomorrow’s two expected counts still add to 768.

संकेत। पहले स्तंभ में उन यात्रियों को देखें जो आज A पर हैं। कल उनमें से 3/4 फिर A पर और 1/4 B पर होंगे।

Find the part that stays and the part that shrinks

det(M − λI) = (3/4 − λ)(1/2 − λ) − 1/8 = (λ − 1)(λ − 1/4).

For λ = 1, a vector that stays fixed is (2, 1)T. Its entries give a 2:1 split. With 768 passengers that means (512, 256)T. For λ = 1/4, multiply M by (1, −1)T to get (1/4, −1/4)T. This direction moves passengers between routes and preserves the total.

Predict day two

Day zero is (768, 0)T = (512, 256)T + 256(1, −1)T. After n weekdays, only the second part changes.

An = 512 + 256(1/4)n   Bn = 256 − 256(1/4)n

For n = 2 the remaining imbalance is 16. The expected counts are 528 on A and 240 on B. As a check, day one is (576, 192). Then 3/4 × 576 + 1/2 × 192 = 528 on A.

Try a different starting split

Keep the same transfer matrix and the same 768 passengers. On day zero, 384 use each route. Draw two columns for A and B before starting the questions.

  1. Where will the passengers be on day one? Check that your answer preserves the total.
  2. Find the coefficient k in (384, 384)T = (512, 256)T + k(1, −1)T. Use it to predict day two.
  3. What is the first whole day on which A is within two passengers of 512, counting a difference of exactly two?
  4. If the number of passengers changed to 900 but the transfer fractions stayed the same, what stationary split would you expect?

खुद जाँचें। यात्रियों की कुल संख्या नहीं बदलती। हर दिन A की संख्या और संतुलन वाली संख्या के बीच का अंतर पहले का एक चौथाई रह जाता है।

Worked answers

1. A gets 3/4 × 384 + 1/2 × 384 = 480. B gets 96 + 192 = 288. The sum is 768.

2. k = 384 − 512 = −128. After two days the coefficient is −128/16 = −8. The counts are (504, 264).

3. The absolute differences on days 0, 1, 2 and 3 are 128, 32, 8 and 2. The first qualifying day is day 3.

4. The fixed vector still has ratio 2:1, so x = (600, 300)T. Multiplying by M returns the same pair.

What would change in real life? These are expected counts when passengers choose probabilistically. A fractional prediction later in the sequence is a mean over many possible days.
